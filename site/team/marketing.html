How these are measured

Airbnb pipeline
Airbnb's own booking conversion numbers for all listings, logged once a month on the Log numbers page (Airbnb → Performance → Conversion → Booking conversion, dates set to that month). Search-to-listing: share of search results where the guest opened our listing. Listing-to-booking: share of listing views that became a booking. Overall conversion: bookings ÷ search appearances, the two combined. First-page impression rate: how often we appear on the first page of results. Similar listings are Airbnb's comparison group. Bookings are counted from Airbnb's monthly performance reports.
Vrbo pipeline
From Vrbo's Ranking metrics for each property, copied monthly on the Log numbers page. Each entry covers the last 30 days, so one entry a month per property adds up to the months in the time range. Viewed = property views ÷ search impressions; booked = bookings ÷ property views; overall = bookings ÷ search impressions.
Furnished Finder pipeline
From Furnished Finder's Listing Performance panel, copied monthly on the Log numbers page. Impressions and listing views cover the last 90 days. Booking inquiries, direct messages, phone number reveals and tenant leads are totals since the listing started, so each month's new ones are the difference from the previous entry (they can overlap: one tenant may message and call). Inquiry → lease = Furnished Finder leases logged ÷ new booking inquiries in the same months. Cost per lease = the yearly listing fee ÷ Furnished Finder leases in the last 12 months. Furnished Finder has no export or API, so these are copied by hand.
Channel value
Revenue share = a channel's booking value ÷ all booking value in the time range, counted by the nights in each month. Fee % = what the channel costs ÷ the booking value it brought: platform fees for Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com (from their exports), Furnished Finder's yearly listing fee spread evenly over the months (a third per home). Direct bookings have no platform fee, so they're left out of Fee %. The monthly line shows changes such as Airbnb moving hosts from its 3% split fee to the 15.5% host-only fee. Furnished Finder and direct leases count only when their monthly rent is logged. Amounts are never shown.
Monthly report runs
On the 1st of each month an n8n workflow computes last month's numbers in code, has Claude write the summary, and blocks the draft unless every number in it appears in the computed data (and it has no dollar amounts). The owner reviews and can edit it; an edit is checked again before it's sent. Each model call is logged with its tokens, cost and time; each outcome (sent, not sent, blocked) is logged too.
Bookings by channel
Stays by check-in (or move-in) month, and nights in each month. Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com come from their exports; Furnished Finder and other bookings from the Log numbers page.
