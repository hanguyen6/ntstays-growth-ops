For bookings from Furnished Finder, our website, referrals, repeat guests or insurance and housing companies. Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com stays come from their exports, so don't add those here.

Furnished Finder listing numbers

Once a month, on about the same day, for each of the three listings: Furnished Finder → Dashboard → Listing Performance (it shows one listing at a time). Pick the home, then copy every number as shown. "Activity in the last 90 days" covers 90 days; the others are totals since the listing started, so the dashboard works out each month's new inquiries from the difference. Leave a box empty if it isn't shown.

Airbnb booking conversion

Airbnb → Performance → Conversion → Booking conversion. Keep "All listings" (or pick one home's listings there and here, for a closer look), set the dates to one whole month (e.g. Sep 1 to Sep 30), and copy the four rates at the top. For the comparison, set "Compare" to "Similar listings" and copy their overall rate. You can go back and add past months too. Logging a month again updates only the boxes you fill in; empty boxes keep what was there.

Vrbo ranking metrics

Once a month, for each property on Vrbo: pick the property at the top of Vrbo's host dashboard, then Ranking metrics. Copy the three numbers under the chart (they cover the last 30 days). Logging a home again in the same month updates it.
