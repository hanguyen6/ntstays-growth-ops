Questions

Common questions

How long can I stay?

As long as you need. We're flexible: we host short stays of a few weeks between contracts, standard 13-week assignments, and much longer stays. Tell us your dates and we'll make it work whenever the home is free.

What if my contract is extended?

Tell us as early as you can. If the home is free for the extra weeks, we'll do our best to extend your stay.

Is it furnished?

Yes. Bedrooms are furnished and each home has a full kitchen, so you can move in with just your suitcase. Ask us if you need anything specific.

How do I pay?

Once we've confirmed your dates, we email you a secure card payment link. Your stay is confirmed as soon as the payment goes through, and you get a confirmation email right away. We never ask for wire transfers, gift cards or crypto.

Can I see the home first?

Yes: every photo of our homes on this site is real, and we have video tours of the Cranston and Abington homes on our homes page. We're happy to set up a video call too.
