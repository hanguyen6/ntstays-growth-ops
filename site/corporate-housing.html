Questions

Common questions

Can a team stay together?

Yes. Our homes have 3 or 4 bedrooms and sleep 6 to 8, so several colleagues can share a home and still have their own bedrooms.

How long can we stay?

As long as you need. We host stays of a few weeks for a project and much longer stays for relocations. Tell us your dates and we'll make it work whenever the home is free.

Is it furnished?

Yes. Bedrooms are furnished and each home has a full kitchen, so you can move in with just a suitcase. Ask us if you need anything specific.

How do we pay?

Once we've confirmed your dates, we email a secure card payment link. The stay is confirmed as soon as the payment goes through, with a confirmation email right away. We never ask for wire transfers, gift cards or crypto.

Can I see the home first?

Yes: every photo of our homes on this site is real, and we have video tours of the Cranston and Abington homes on our homes page. We're happy to set up a video call too.
