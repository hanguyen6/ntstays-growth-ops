Campaign links

Use a tagged link wherever you share the website: a post, an email, an ad, a flyer. When someone sends a request after clicking it, their contact in HubSpot shows the source and campaign, and the owner email says where they came from.

For print: put this QR code on the flyer or card. Scans count like clicks.

Naming rules

RuleWhy
Lowercase, words joined with - (the builder does this)Facebook and facebook would count as two sources.
Source = the place: facebook, hospital-board, furnished-finderAnswers "where did they see us?"
Campaign = audience + month: nurses-2026-10, adjusters-2026-q4, owners-mailer-2026-11Compare efforts over time.
Version only when you're testing two versions: blue-flyer vs photo-flyerSee which one works better.
Keep a short list of your campaigns (name, dates, what it was, what it cost)So the numbers make sense months later.

What isn't tracked

Someone who clicks a link, leaves, and types ntstays.com days later shows up as "direct": the site only remembers the link for that visit, with no cookies. Word of mouth, calls and repeat guests don't come through links at all; the "How did you hear about us?" question on the request forms covers those.
